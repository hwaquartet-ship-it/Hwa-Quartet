About Hwa String Quartet

Formed in 2025, Hwa String Quartet unites four musicians from Singapore, Korea, Taiwan, and China. 'Hwa (花)' symbolizes flowers in Asian cultures — representing beauty, growth, and connection. The quartet celebrates cultural diversity and musical collaboration, performing repertoire that ranges from classical masterpieces to contemporary works and innovative arrangements.
